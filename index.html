01. About Me

I'm a computer science student at Florida International University, and I build things that people actually use. That constraint shapes how I work: a booking engine has to take a deposit correctly the first time, and a wedding invitation has to render as a link card in iMessage or nobody opens it.

Lately most of my time has gone into AI agents and the question of where a person belongs in the loop. My main project, Human in the Loop, is a research agent that stops and shows you the bill before it spends anything, then proves its citations were really retrieved rather than remembered — a check enforced in code, not asked for in a prompt.

The rest of my work runs from full-stack FastAPI and React applications down to serverless functions on AWS and C coursework on CPU scheduling. I like the parts other people skip: migrations, boundary cases, and making a project run on a fresh laptop with no API keys and no network.
